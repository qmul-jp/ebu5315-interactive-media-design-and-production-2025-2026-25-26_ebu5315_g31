Inscribed angle theorem

The essential rules

Four ideas unlock most circle problems.

Each rule links a visible relationship to a calculation you can test in the lab.

01

Central angle

A central angle has the same measure as its intercepted arc.

∠AOB = ⌢AB
02

Inscribed angle

An angle at the circumference is half the angle at the centre.

∠ACB = ½∠AOB
03

Thales' theorem

A triangle drawn on a diameter always contains a right angle.

∠ACB = 90°
04

Tangent theorem

A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.

OT ⟂ ℓT
